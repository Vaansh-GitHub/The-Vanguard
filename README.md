# The Vanguard Game ⚔️

## Story 📖
<p>
    In the mystical realm of Ashenwood 🌲, a dark shadow has fallen. The ancient peace is shattered as evil forces—skeletons 💀, goblins 👺, and corrupted flora 🍄—rise to claim the land.
</p>
<p>
    You are the Vanguard 🛡️, the last hope of Ashenwood.
</p>
<p>
    Embark on a perilous journey across distinct realms 🗺️. Battle through hordes of enemies, solve ancient puzzles 🧩, and confront the dread Lords 👑 who guard the source of the corruption.
</p>
<p>
    With bravery and skill, can you purge the evil and restore light ✨ to the world?
    The fate of Ashenwood rests in your hands ✋.
</p>

## How to Play 🎮
<p>Master these controls to save Ashenwood:</p>
<p><strong>A or Right Arrow</strong> : Move Left ⬅️</p>
<p><strong>D or Left Arrow</strong> : Move Right ➡️</p>
<p><strong>W or Up Arrow</strong> : Jump / Double Jump ⬆️</p>
<p><strong>Space</strong> : Attack ⚔️</p>
